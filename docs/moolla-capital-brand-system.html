Brand Voice
⚖️
Authoritative
We speak with decades of expertise behind every word. Confident without arrogance — grounded in evidence and track record.
Example
"Our integrated approach ensures regulatory compliance while accelerating your wealth objectives."
🤝
Trustworthy
We are transparent, accountable, and human. Jargon is used only when necessary — always explained clearly.
Example
"You deal with one trusted firm for all your financial needs — from auditing to offshore investment."
🌍
Globally Minded
We think beyond borders. Language reflects international sophistication without losing our South African identity.
Example
"Build wealth beyond borders — we navigate international structures so you don't have to."
🎯
Precise
Every word earns its place. We are direct, specific, and concise — never vague, never over-promising.
Example
"Our offshore structures are fully SARS-compliant and optimised for long-term capital preservation."
✓ Do
  • Use confident, clear language that projects expertise
  • Lead with client outcomes, not service features
  • Reference 30+ years of heritage when relevant
  • Pair serif headlines with clean body copy
  • Use gold as an accent — not a primary fill
✗ Don't
  • Use casual slang or exclamation marks in copy
  • Fill backgrounds with gold — it loses its premium signal
  • Use more than two typefaces in a single layout
  • Use red as anything other than an error/alert state
  • Make promises around specific returns or guarantees
